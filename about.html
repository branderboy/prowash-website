Our Story

FROM VISION TO 5 LOCATIONS

Hand Car Wash & Car Detail is a professional hand wash and auto detail center offering the time efficiency of an express facility with the quality and swirl-free paint results of a full service wash.

We offer an array of service packages from basic full service washes to the extensive master detail package. We also provide professional consultation and education on paint and interior car preservation.

What started as a mobile detailing operation has grown into five full-service locations across the Washington DC and Maryland area, serving thousands of satisfied customers every month.

"Early on I recognized a need for mobile full service washing and car detailing. I built this business to fill that gap — delivering quality that speaks for itself."

— Lemond B., Business Owner
