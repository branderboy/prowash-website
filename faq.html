UNDERSTANDING AUTO CARE

New to professional auto care? No worries. Here's what you need to know: straight talk, no fluff.

A hand car wash is when trained technicians wash your vehicle by hand. No machines, no spinning brushes. We use microfiber mitts, professional-grade soap, and clean water to carefully clean every surface. That means your body panels, wheels, tires, windows, door jambs, and all the tight spots automated washes completely miss. Hand washing provides a deeper, more detailed clean that protects your paint from swirl marks and micro-scratches, while keeping your car's value intact.
Car detailing is a deep cleaning and restoration of your vehicle, way beyond a regular wash. Think of it as a full reset. Interior detailing means deep vacuuming, steam cleaning carpets, conditioning leather, removing stains, and eliminating odors. Exterior detailing includes clay bar treatment, polishing, wax or sealant application, and paint correction. The goal is to make your car look and feel like it just came off the lot. A wash maintains your car; a detail restores it.
Window tinting is applying a thin film to the inside of your car windows. It blocks up to 99% of harmful UV rays, reduces heat by up to 60%, adds privacy and security, and gives your vehicle a sleek, customized look. Professional tinting with quality film won't bubble, peel, or turn purple like cheap DIY kits. Our installations come with a lifetime warranty. If it ever fails, we fix it free.
Hand washing provides superior, detailed cleaning that protects paint from swirl marks, while automated washes offer speed and convenience at lower costs. Brush-type automated washes can damage clear coats with micro-scratches over time. Hand washing is best for maintaining car value, protecting paint, and cleaning hard-to-reach areas like wheels and crevices. If you care about your car's long-term appearance and resale value, hand washing is the smarter choice, especially for luxury cars, ceramic coatings, and newer vehicles.
Most experts recommend washing every 2 weeks (or weekly if you park outside or drive a lot). For detailing, every 3-6 months keeps your interior and exterior in top condition. Regular care prevents dirt, road salt, bird droppings, and tree sap from permanently damaging your paint. Think of it like regular maintenance: consistent care prevents expensive problems down the road.
