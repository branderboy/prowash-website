General Questions

We have 5 locations across the DMV area: Clinton, MD (7403 Surratts Rd), Capitol Heights, MD (1016 Hampton Park Blvd), Bowie, MD (4406 NW Crain Hwy), Upper Marlboro, MD (10026 Trade Zone Ave), and Washington, DC (4714 Kenilworth Ave NE). Visit our locations page for maps and directions.
Most locations are open Monday through Saturday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Some locations have slightly different hours (for example, Bowie closes at 5:30 PM on Fridays and Saturdays). We are closed on Sundays. Check your specific location page for exact hours.
Walk-ins are always welcome at every location for car wash services. For detailing and window tinting, we recommend calling ahead at (301) 307-1414 or booking online to secure your preferred time, especially on weekends.
We accept cash, all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay, and Google Pay at all locations.
Yes! We offer a loyalty card program where you earn a stamp with every wash. After 8 stamps, your next wash is free. We also have a Birthday Club with exclusive discounts and offers. Ask any team member for details or visit our Rewards page.

Car Wash Services

Our basic exterior wash starts at $15 for cars, $20 for trucks/SUVs, and $25 for oversized vehicles. Full service washes (exterior + interior) range from $25 to $45, and our premium deluxe wash runs $40 to $60 depending on vehicle size. See our full car wash pricing for all packages.
A basic exterior wash takes about 15-20 minutes. A full service wash with interior cleaning typically takes 30-45 minutes. Wait times may vary depending on how busy the location is.
100% hand washed, every vehicle, every time. We never use automated brushes or tunnel systems. Our team uses professional-grade microfiber materials and products to deliver a swirl-free finish that machines simply cannot match.
Absolutely. We wash vehicles of all sizes including sedans, trucks, SUVs, vans, and oversized vehicles. Pricing varies by vehicle size category: car, truck/SUV, and oversized.

Auto Detailing

We offer several packages: Super Sport ($95-$115), Express ($120-$155), Sport ($155-$195), Premium ($175-$225), Deluxe ($225-$275), Supreme ($255-$295), and our top-tier Master Detail ($295-$355). Each package includes different levels of interior and exterior treatment. View our full detailing menu for details.
Express details typically take 1-2 hours. Mid-range packages like Sport or Premium take 2-4 hours. Our full Master Detail can take 4-8 hours depending on vehicle size and condition. We recommend leaving your vehicle with us and picking it up when we call.
The Master Detail ($295 cars, $355 trucks) is our most comprehensive package. It includes full exterior wash and decontamination, clay bar treatment, paint correction with buff and wax, complete interior deep clean with steam, leather and vinyl conditioning, window and glass treatment inside and out, tire and wheel dressing, and a final quality inspection.
Yes. Our paint correction service, included in higher-tier packages and available as an add-on, uses professional buffing and polishing techniques to remove or significantly reduce scratches, swirl marks, and oxidation. For severe damage, we'll provide an honest assessment of what we can achieve.

Window Tinting

Two-door vehicles start at $59.95 and four-door vehicles start at $108. Pricing varies by film type and vehicle size. We offer a range of films including dyed, carbon, and ceramic options. Visit our window tinting page for full pricing.
In Maryland, front side windows must allow at least 35% visible light transmission (VLT). Rear side windows and the back window can be any shade. In Washington DC, front side windows must allow 70% VLT, while rear windows can be any shade. Our technicians will help you choose a legal tint that looks great.
Yes, all of our window tint installations come with a lifetime warranty covering bubbling, peeling, and fading. If anything goes wrong with the film, bring it back and we'll take care of it at no charge.
Most tint jobs take 1-3 hours depending on the number of windows and vehicle type. We recommend leaving your vehicle with us. After installation, the tint needs 3-5 days to fully cure. Avoid rolling down tinted windows during this period.
