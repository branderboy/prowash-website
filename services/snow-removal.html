Why Go Professional?

WHY PROFESSIONAL SNOW REMOVAL?

DIY Snow Removal Is a Liability Risk

When snow and ice accumulate on your property, you are responsible for what happens there. A slip-and-fall on your uncleared sidewalk or driveway can expose you to serious legal liability. Professional snow removal means your property is cleared properly and promptly, reducing your risk significantly.

Proper Equipment Makes All the Difference

Consumer-grade shovels and snow blowers are no match for heavy accumulations, packed ice, or large commercial lots. Our professional-grade plows, spreaders, and equipment handle any snowfall efficiently so your property is cleared fast without damage to pavement or landscaping.

Road Salt Applied Wrong Destroys Concrete and Landscaping

Over-salting or using the wrong de-icing product damages concrete, kills grass and plants, and corrodes metal. Our crews apply the right products in the right amounts, protecting your property while keeping surfaces safe.

Rapid Response When It Matters Most

When a snowstorm hits overnight, you need crews ready to clear before morning. Our priority scheduling and emergency response options mean your parking lot, loading dock, or business entrance is cleared and safe before your doors open.

Got Questions?

SNOW REMOVAL FAQ

What is included in professional snow removal?

Our professional snow removal service includes snow plowing of driveways, parking lots, and access roads, salting and de-icing to prevent black ice and refreezing, and hand-shoveling of sidewalks, walkways, and entry areas. Depending on your package, we also provide pre-treatment before storms and follow-up salting after clearing. Every visit is documented so you have a record of service.

How quickly can you respond after a snowfall?

Response time depends on your service package and the severity of the storm. Priority and emergency customers are dispatched first and typically cleared within 2 to 4 hours of snowfall ending, or during the storm for heavy accumulations. Standard customers are serviced same day. Seasonal contract customers get guaranteed response windows and are always scheduled ahead of one-time service requests.

Do you offer seasonal contracts?

Yes. We offer seasonal contracts that cover all snowfall events for the entire winter season at a fixed price. Seasonal pricing gives you budget predictability and priority scheduling over per-event customers. We also offer flexible per-visit pricing if you only want service for specific storms. Contact us to discuss what works best for your property and budget.

What types of commercial properties do you service?

We service a wide range of commercial properties across the DMV area including office parks, retail centers, shopping plazas, warehouses, loading docks, access roads, fire lanes, and building entrances. We have equipment scaled for small business lots all the way up to large commercial campuses and multi-building complexes. Contact us to discuss your specific property.

Do I need to be home during service?

No, you do not need to be on-site for service. For commercial properties, we set up access arrangements in advance so our crews can work independently. You will receive a service notification when your property has been cleared so you always know the status without having to wait around. Most of our commercial clients simply receive a confirmation and arrive to a clear, safe property.
