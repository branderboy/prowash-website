What We Do

PROFESSIONAL SNOW & ICE MANAGEMENT

Pro Snow™ is the commercial snow removal division of Hand Car Wash & Car Detail. We handle snow plowing, salting, ice management, and sidewalk clearing for commercial properties across Washington DC and Maryland. The same reliability and professionalism you know from our car care services, applied to keeping your property safe and accessible during winter storms.

More Than Just Plowing

We provide full-service winter weather management. That means pre-treatment with salt and brine before the storm hits, snow plowing for parking lots and driveways, sidewalk shoveling, ice melt application on walkways and entrances, and post-storm cleanup. Your property stays safe from the first flake to the last pile.

Available When You Need Us

We monitor weather forecasts and mobilize crews before storms hit. By the time your employees and customers arrive, your parking lots are plowed, your sidewalks are cleared, and your entrances are treated. Your business opens on time, every time.

24/7 Storm Response

EMERGENCY SNOW REMOVAL

When unexpected storms hit or accumulation exceeds forecasts, we deploy additional crews to keep your property safe. Properties under contract get priority service, but we also handle emergency one-time requests as capacity allows.

Rapid Response Times

Our crews can be on-site within hours of storm onset for contracted properties. We pre-position equipment and materials based on weather forecasts so we're ready to roll the moment snow starts falling. For emergency one-time service, we respond as quickly as capacity allows.

Lock in your rates and guarantee priority service with a seasonal contract. Includes pre-treatment, plowing, sidewalks, and ice management for the entire winter season. No surprises, no scrambling for a plow when the snow hits. One contract, total coverage, all winter long.
