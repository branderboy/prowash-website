Stay Ahead of the Game

THE RIDESHARE DRIVER'S DETAILING SCHEDULE

Top-rated drivers don't wait until their car looks dirty. They follow a consistent maintenance schedule that keeps their vehicle in 5-star condition at all times. Here is the schedule we recommend to maintain a 4.9+ rating.

Weekly: Exterior Wash

A full exterior hand wash every week removes road grime, bird droppings, and pollen that passengers notice the moment they approach your car. Starting at $15, it is the most cost-effective way to keep your rating high and your tips coming in.

Bi-Weekly: Interior Vacuum

Every two weeks, get a thorough interior vacuum to remove crumbs, dirt, and debris left by passengers. Clean floor mats and seats make a huge difference in how riders perceive your vehicle from the moment they sit down.

Monthly: Express Detail

Once a month, invest in an express detail that includes interior steam cleaning, dashboard conditioning, and window clarity treatment. This resets your interior to near-new condition and eliminates lingering odors from hundreds of passenger trips.

Quarterly: Deep Detail

Every three months, schedule a full deep detail with leather conditioning, paint correction, stain extraction, and odor treatment. This protects your vehicle's long-term value, prevents permanent wear, and keeps you at the top of the rating leaderboard.

Rating Factors

WHAT RIDERS NOTICE (AND RATE YOU ON)

Passengers form their opinion of your vehicle in the first 10 seconds. Here are the cleanliness factors that directly impact your star rating and how we address each one.

Smell

Nothing kills a rating faster than a bad smell. Our washes include air freshener application, and our details use enzyme-based odor elimination to neutralize food, smoke, and body odors at the source.

Seat Stains

Visible stains on seats make passengers uncomfortable immediately. Our steam cleaning and stain extraction process removes coffee spills, food marks, and mystery stains so your seats look clean and inviting every ride.

Floor Mats

Dirty, stained floor mats are one of the first things passengers see when they step in. We vacuum, shampoo, and treat your floor mats to remove ground-in dirt and restore them to a clean, professional appearance.

Dashboard Dust

A dusty dashboard screams neglect. Every wash includes a full dashboard and console wipe-down with UV-protective conditioner that keeps surfaces clean and prevents cracking from sun exposure.

Window Clarity

Smudged or hazy windows reduce visibility and look unprofessional. We clean all windows inside and out with streak-free products so your passengers have a crystal-clear view and feel like they are in a well-maintained vehicle.

Tire Appearance

Riders notice curb appeal before they even get in. Tire dressing gives your wheels a clean, glossy look that signals professionalism. It is included with every wash so your car always looks sharp from the outside in.
