Got Questions?

WHAT IS A HAND CAR WASH?

What is a hand car wash?

A hand car wash is exactly what it sounds like. real people washing your car by hand, not machines. Our trained technicians use microfiber mitts, quality soap, and clean water to carefully wash every inch of your vehicle. That means your body panels, wheels, tires, windows, door jambs, mirrors, and all the spots automated washes completely miss. It's the safest, most thorough way to clean your vehicle without damaging the paint.

Hand wash vs. automatic car wash. what's the real difference?

Hand washing provides a deeper, more detailed clean that actually protects your paint from swirl marks and micro-scratches. Automated washes. especially brush-type. drag trapped dirt across your clear coat every time, dulling and damaging it over time. Hand washing is better for your wheels, crevices, trim, and overall vehicle value. Automatic washes are faster and cheaper, but if you care about maintaining your car's appearance and resale value, hand washing is the smarter choice. That's the trade-off: convenience vs. quality.

Is hand washing better for my car's paint?

Yes. Hand washing is the best option for protecting your car's paint, especially if you have a ceramic coating, wax protection, or a luxury/new vehicle. Soft mitts and careful technique mean zero swirl marks, zero brush scratches, and zero clear coat damage. It's why enthusiasts, car collectors, and dealerships trust hand washes over machines every time.

How long does a hand car wash take?

About 20 minutes for a full-service wash at our facility. That includes the exterior hand wash, interior vacuum, window cleaning, and tire dressing. Express-speed service with none of the machine damage. Walk-ins welcome, no appointment needed.

How often should I wash my car?

Most experts recommend every 2 weeks, or weekly if you drive a lot, park outside, or live near heavy traffic. Regular washing prevents dirt, road salt, bird droppings, and tree sap from eating into your paint. Think of it like brushing your teeth. consistent care prevents expensive problems down the road. Our loyalty program makes regular washes more affordable too.

Do I need an appointment?
