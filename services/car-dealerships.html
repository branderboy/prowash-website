Our Process

HOW DEALERSHIP SERVICES WORK

1. Initial Consultation

We visit your dealership to assess your lot size, vehicle volume, and current wash operations. This helps us understand your specific needs, pain points, and budget so we can design the right solution from day one.

2. Custom Service Plan

Based on our assessment, we create a tailored service plan that covers lot washing frequency, detail services, staffing levels, and pricing. Every plan is built around your dealership's workflow and sales cycle.

3. Scheduled Lot Washing

Our crew arrives on schedule to rinse, wipe, and dry every vehicle on your lot. Whether it is daily, twice a week, or weekly, your inventory stays spotless and presentation-ready for every customer who walks your lot.

4. Detail on Demand

Need a trade-in reconditioned for the sales floor or a new delivery prepped for a customer? Just call. Our team handles on-demand detail requests with fast turnaround so your sales team never has to wait.

5. Quality Reporting

We provide regular service reports documenting completed work, vehicle counts, and any issues identified. You always know exactly what was done, when, and by whom. Full transparency keeps your operation running smoothly.

6. Flexible Billing

Choose per-vehicle pricing, flat monthly rates, or a hybrid model. We offer net-30 invoicing for established accounts and work with your accounting team to make billing seamless and predictable.
