The Basics

WHAT IS CAR DETAILING?

Car detailing is a deep, thorough cleaning and restoration of your vehicle — far beyond what a regular car wash does. Think of a car wash as brushing your teeth. A detail is like going to the dentist for a full cleaning.

During a detail, a technician works through your entire vehicle — inside and out. On the exterior, that means removing embedded dirt from the paint, treating scratches and oxidation, and applying wax or sealant to protect the finish. On the interior, it means deep cleaning the carpets, steam cleaning the seats, conditioning the leather, scrubbing the dashboard and vents, and eliminating odors at the source.

Car Wash vs. Car Detailing — What's the Difference?

A car wash cleans the surface. It removes dirt, dust, and light grime from the outside of your car and gives the interior a quick vacuum. Takes about 20 minutes. Starts at $15.

A car detail goes deeper. It removes things a wash can't — baked-in brake dust, tree sap, paint oxidation, ground-in stains, pet hair embedded in fabric, and smells trapped in the upholstery. It restores the car closer to how it looked when it was new. Takes 1–3 hours. Starts at $95.

Simple rule: Get a wash every 2 weeks to maintain your car. Get a detail 1–2 times a year to restore it. If you're selling your car, returning a lease, or your interior smells — you need a detail, not a wash.

Which Detail Package Is Right for You?

Super Sport ($95–$125): Best for regular upkeep. Includes hand wax, leather conditioning, steam clean floor mats, and deodorizer. Good for cars that are already in decent shape and just need a refresh.

Steam Clean ($150–$175): Best for interior-focused jobs. High-temperature steam kills bacteria, lifts stains from fabric and carpet, and eliminates odors that air fresheners only cover up. Great for family cars, pet owners, or smokers.

Express Detail ($185–$210): Best for a complete reset. Full interior and exterior treatment without the multi-hour commitment. If your car needs more than a wash but you don't need the full master package.

Buff Wax ($175–$215): Best for paint correction. Removes oxidation, minor scratches, and swirl marks with machine buffing, then seals with a hand-applied carnauba wax. For cars with dull or faded paint.

Master Detail ($295–$355): The full treatment. Everything from every other package combined — interior steam clean, buff wax, engine steam clean, leather conditioning, and full paint restoration. Best for pre-sale prep, lease returns, or vehicles that haven't been detailed in over a year.

AUTO DETAILING: YOUR QUESTIONS ANSWERED

What is car detailing and what does it include?

Car detailing is a deep, systematic cleaning and restoration of your vehicle — far beyond what a standard car wash covers. It typically includes paint decontamination, clay bar treatment, machine polishing or wax, interior steam cleaning, leather conditioning, odor elimination, and trim restoration.

Our packages range from a focused Super Sport Detail ($95–$125) up to the comprehensive Master Detail ($295–$355) — the closest thing to a full vehicle restoration short of a body shop.

Is detailing worth it? When should I get my car detailed?

Detailing is worth it when surface cleaning no longer gets the job done, or when you want to actively protect and maintain your vehicle's condition. Get a detail:

  • Before selling or trading in your vehicle — detailing can increase resale value by $500–$2,000
  • When returning a leased vehicle to avoid wear-and-tear charges
  • Once or twice a year for general maintenance and paint protection
  • When there are persistent odors, stains, or visible oxidation on your paint
  • For rideshare drivers (Uber/Lyft) who need a consistently clean, professional vehicle
