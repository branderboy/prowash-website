WHAT IS CAR DETAILING?

What is car detailing exactly?

Car detailing is a deep, thorough cleaning and restoration of your vehicle, way beyond a normal car wash. It involves meticulous cleaning, polishing, and protecting every surface inside and out. Interior detailing covers deep vacuuming, steam cleaning, leather conditioning, stain removal, and odor elimination. Exterior detailing includes clay bar treatment, polish, wax or sealant, and paint correction. The goal is to make your car look and feel like it just rolled off the lot.

What's the difference between a car wash and a detail?

A car wash cleans the surface: soap, rinse, dry, done in 20 minutes. A detail goes deep. We're talking about getting into every seam, vent, crevice, and fiber. Steam cleaning carpets, conditioning leather, removing water spots, polishing paint, and applying protective coatings. A wash maintains your car. A detail restores it. Most people need a wash every 2 weeks and a full detail every 3-6 months.

How long does a car detail take?

It depends on the package. An express detail takes about 45-60 minutes. A master detail can take 2-3 hours because we're covering everything: interior deep clean, exterior buff and wax, engine bay, the works. We complete all detail jobs between 8 AM and 4:30 PM so your car is ready same-day.

How often should I detail my car?

Every 3-6 months is ideal for most vehicles. If you have kids, pets, eat in your car, or park outside regularly, consider every 2-3 months. Regular detailing protects your interior from UV damage, keeps leather from cracking, maintains paint condition, and honestly, it just makes driving more enjoyable when your car is clean.

Is detailing worth the money?

Absolutely. A properly detailed car can add hundreds, sometimes thousands, to your resale value. Beyond money, it prevents long-term damage like cracked leather, faded paint, permanent stains, and musty odors. It's maintenance, not luxury. The same way you service your engine, you should service the surfaces you actually see and touch every day.

Can you detail my car at my home or office?

Yes! All of our detail packages are available as mobile service. We come to your home, office, or anywhere in the DMV area. There's just a $10 dispatch fee added to any service. Book your mobile detail here.
