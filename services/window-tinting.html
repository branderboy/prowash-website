The Basics

WHAT IS WINDOW TINTING?

Window tinting is a thin film applied to the inside of your car's windows. It darkens the glass, blocks UV rays, reduces heat inside the cabin, and gives your car a cleaner, more finished look. The film is applied by a certified installer using a heat gun and precision cutting tools — it bonds directly to the glass and lasts for years.

At ProWash, every tint installation comes with a lifetime warranty and a free car wash. Our installers are certified and use industry-leading film that won't bubble, peel, or turn purple over time.

Why Tint Your Windows?

UV protection: Window tint blocks up to 99% of harmful UV rays. This protects your skin during long commutes and prevents your dashboard, seats, and trim from fading and cracking over time.

Heat reduction: Tinted windows keep your car noticeably cooler in summer. Less heat means less AC, which means better fuel economy. In the DMV's July humidity, you'll feel the difference immediately.

Privacy and security: Darker windows make it harder for people to see inside your car. That means your belongings are less visible when parked — reducing the chance of break-ins.

Glare reduction: Tint cuts down on sun glare while driving, which reduces eye strain — especially during sunrise and sunset commutes on the Beltway or 495.

Professional Tint vs. DIY Tint Kits

You can buy tint film online and try to install it yourself. Most people who do end up with bubbles, creases, and peeling edges within a few months. The film quality is lower, and without the right tools and technique, it's almost impossible to get a clean finish — especially on curved rear windows.

Professional tinting uses higher-grade film, precision cutting, and heat-shrinking techniques that make the tint conform perfectly to your glass. Our installations come with a lifetime warranty — if it ever bubbles, peels, or fades, we replace it free.
