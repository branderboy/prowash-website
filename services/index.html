The Facts

HAND WASH VS. AUTOMATIC CAR WASH

Not all car washes are equal. Here's what the research shows — and why Pro Wash™ is the smarter choice for your vehicle's long-term health.

Recommended

Pro Wash™ Hand Car Wash

  • Superior paint protection — no abrasive brushes, zero swirl marks or micro-scratches to your clear coat
  • Thorough cleaning of crevices — door jambs, trim lines, mirrors, and tight spots that machines miss entirely
  • Trained eyes on your vehicle — our technicians spot paint issues, chips, and problem areas as they work
  • Interior included — vacuum, window cleaning, and tire dressing with every wash package
  • Still fast — in and out in 20 minutes. Express speed without machine damage
The Alternative

Automatic Car Wash

  • Brush-type machines damage clear coats — repeated use causes micro-scratches and swirl marks that dull paint over time
  • Misses crevices entirely — automated nozzles can't reach door jambs, trim, mirrors, or tight body contours
  • No interior service — exterior only; vacuuming, windows, and tires are left to you
  • Convenient for a quick underbody rinse — best when time is critical and paint condition isn't a priority

The Verdict

"Hand washing is superior for paint protection and thorough cleaning. Automatic washes (especially brush-type) can damage clear coats with micro-scratches over time." — Google
