ABOUT OUR WASHINGTON DC LOCATION

Our Washington DC hand car wash is located at 1329 Kenilworth Ave NE in the heart of Northeast Washington, DC. Situated in the Deanwood-Kenilworth corridor, we serve drivers throughout NE DC, Deanwood, Kenilworth, Capitol Hill, Anacostia, Benning Ridge, and the H Street Corridor. Whether you are heading home after work or running weekend errands, our Kenilworth Ave location offers easy access from Eastern Ave, DC-295, and the surrounding NE DC street grid — no need to drive across the District or cross into Maryland for a quality hand car wash.

We are just minutes from some of the district's best-known landmarks including the National Arboretum, Kenilworth Aquatic Gardens, and the RFK Stadium area. Our car wash in Washington DC was built to serve the city's government workers, Uber and Lyft drivers, daily commuters, and anyone who wants a professional hand wash without the hassle of a long drive. As the only DC location in our network, this facility is designed for quick, convenient auto detailing service for the city's busy drivers.

From a basic exterior hand car wash starting at $15 to full auto detailing packages, window tinting, and mobile car wash service across DC, our Washington DC team delivers the same professional quality and attention to detail that has made us a trusted name in the DMV. Walk in anytime Monday through Saturday — no appointment needed for car washes.
