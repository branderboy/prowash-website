ABOUT OUR BOWIE LOCATION

Our Bowie car wash is conveniently located at 4406 NW Crain Hwy (US-301) in the heart of the Bowie area, making it easy to spot and perfect for a quick drive-by visit. Whether you are coming from Crofton, Mitchellville, Glenn Dale, Lanham, or Kettering, our hand car wash and auto detailing center is just minutes away. With quick access from US-50, US-301, and the Capital Beltway (I-495/I-95), drivers across Prince George's County and Anne Arundel County can reach us without going out of their way.

Our Bowie MD car wash sits near Bowie Town Center, Free State Shopping Center, and Allen Pond Park, so you can easily combine your car wash with shopping, dining, or a trip to the park. Most hand car washes take about 20 minutes, giving you plenty of time to run a quick errand and return to a spotless vehicle. We also offer mobile car wash and auto detailing service throughout Bowie and the surrounding communities if you prefer we come directly to your home or office.

Bowie is one of Maryland's largest cities and a hub for families, professionals, and commuters who depend on reliable vehicles every day. We are proud to keep Bowie's cars looking their best with careful, hand-wash-only service that protects your paint and finish. Whether you need a quick exterior car wash, a comprehensive interior and exterior detail, or professional window tinting with a lifetime warranty, our trained technicians at the Bowie location are here to help you maintain your vehicle's appearance and value.
