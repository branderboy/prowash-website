ABOUT OUR CAPITOL HEIGHTS LOCATION

Our Capitol Heights car wash is located on Hampton Park Blvd in the heart of Capitol Heights, Maryland, just steps from Hampton Park Plaza. Whether you live in the neighborhood or you're passing through on your way to or from Washington, DC, our team delivers the same hand car wash quality and personal attention that has made us a trusted name across Prince George's County. We proudly serve drivers from Capitol Heights, District Heights, Seat Pleasant, Fairmount Heights, Landover, and Largo with professional hand car wash, auto detailing, window tinting, and mobile services.

Getting here is easy no matter which direction you're coming from. We're just off Central Ave (MD-214), with quick access from Martin Luther King Jr Hwy and the Capital Beltway (I-495/I-95). If you're heading to or from FedExField, we're a convenient stop to get your vehicle looking its best before a game or event. The Addison Road-Seat Pleasant Metro station on the Blue and Silver lines is also nearby, making it simple to drop your car off for a detail and pick it up later.

Capitol Heights is one of Prince George's County's most accessible communities, sitting right at the DC border. That central location means our car wash Capitol Heights MD facility is an ideal choice for commuters, rideshare drivers, and anyone who wants a spotless vehicle without going out of their way. From a quick $15 exterior wash to a full Master Detail, our trained technicians are here Monday through Saturday to keep your car, truck, or SUV looking showroom-ready.

Common Questions

CAPITOL HEIGHTS CAR WASH FAQ

Where is the Capitol Heights car wash located?+
Our Capitol Heights car wash is located at 29 Hampton Park Blvd, Capitol Heights, MD 20743. We're right off Central Ave (MD-214) near Hampton Park Plaza, with easy access from the Capital Beltway (I-495/I-95) and Martin Luther King Jr Hwy. Look for us in the Hampton Park shopping area.
What are the hours at the Capitol Heights location?+
Our Capitol Heights facility is open Monday through Thursday from 8 AM to 6 PM, and Friday through Saturday from 8 AM to 5 PM. We are closed on Sundays. Our mobile car wash and detailing team operates Monday through Saturday from 8 AM to 6:30 PM with a $10 dispatch fee.
Do I need an appointment for a car wash in Capitol Heights?+
No appointment is needed for any car wash at our Capitol Heights location. Walk-ins are always welcome during business hours, and most washes are finished in about 20 minutes. If you'd like to schedule an auto detail or mobile service, you can book online or call us at (301) 307-1414.
What services are available at Capitol Heights?+
Our Capitol Heights location offers a full menu of auto care services: hand car wash packages starting at $15, five auto detailing packages from $95 to $355, professional window tinting with a lifetime warranty (cars from $108, trucks from $60), and mobile car wash and detailing that comes to your home or office. We service cars, trucks, SUVs, and XL vehicles.
Is there parking while I wait at Capitol Heights?+
Yes, our Capitol Heights facility at 29 Hampton Park Blvd has a comfortable waiting area where you can relax while your vehicle is being washed or detailed. For exterior-only washes, you can stay in your vehicle. There is plenty of parking available in the Hampton Park Plaza lot.
Can I get detailing done in Capitol Heights?+
Absolutely. Our Capitol Heights location offers five auto detail packages: Super Sport ($95 cars), Express Detail, Steam Clean, Buff & Wax, and the Master Detail ($295 cars / $355 trucks). Services include interior deep cleaning, steam cleaning, buff and wax, paint correction, and leather conditioning. Auto detail near DC has never been more convenient. View all detail packages.
Do you serve District Heights and Seat Pleasant?+
Yes, our Capitol Heights car wash proudly serves drivers from District Heights, Seat Pleasant, Fairmount Heights, Landover, Largo, and all surrounding Prince George's County communities. We also offer a mobile car wash and detailing service that comes directly to your home or workplace anywhere in the area for just a $10 dispatch fee.
Is the Capitol Heights location near the Metro?+
Yes, our Capitol Heights car wash is conveniently located near the Addison Road-Seat Pleasant Metro station on the Blue and Silver lines. You can easily drop your vehicle off for a wash or detail and pick it up on your way back from the Metro. We're also just a short drive from the Capitol Heights Metro station.
