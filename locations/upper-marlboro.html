ABOUT OUR UPPER MARLBORO LOCATION

Our Upper Marlboro car wash is located at 7538 NW Crain Hwy (US-301), right in the heart of Prince George's County's county seat. Whether you're heading to the Prince George's County Courthouse for business, spending the day at Six Flags America, or catching an event at The Show Place Arena, our location on Crain Highway makes it easy to get a professional hand car wash on the way. With convenient access from US-301, MD-4 (Pennsylvania Avenue), and MD-382, you're never far from a quality wash and detail.

We proudly serve drivers throughout Upper Marlboro and the surrounding communities, including Marlton, Brandywine, Cheltenham, Forestville, and the Joint Base Andrews area. Our mobile car wash service also covers these neighborhoods — we'll come to your home or office for just a $10 dispatch fee. Whether you need a quick exterior wash before a weekend outing or a full interior detail to restore your ride, our Upper Marlboro team has you covered.

Upper Marlboro combines small-town charm with suburban convenience, and our car wash fits right in. We offer friendly, personalized service with the same professional results you'd expect from a premium detailer. Walk in any time Monday through Saturday — no appointment needed for car washes. For detailing, window tinting, or mobile service, book online or call us at (301) 307-1414.
